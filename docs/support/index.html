Support

Contact support

Email support@fantasylockerapp.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, or private API keys.

Account and privacy

Account deletion can be initiated from within the FantasyLocker app. For privacy questions or data-access requests, email support@fantasylockerapp.com.

Read the Privacy Policy

Fantasy platform imports

FantasyLocker may connect to supported third-party fantasy platforms. Availability and imported history depend on the provider and the data the provider makes available.

Check your build

Include your FantasyLocker version and build number when reporting a problem. If you use TestFlight, include the build shown there.

Report incorrect history

In Career, open the affected season and choose Report a data issue. Include the platform, league, season and what looks incorrect. Reports preserve your current record while the source evidence is reviewed.

Export or remove your data

Open Profile → Settings → Your data → Export profile and history. To remove an import, open Settings → Connected accounts and remove the relevant connection. To permanently delete your account, use Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage renewal in Apple’s subscription settings.

Account verification update

The beta has import connections for Sleeper, Fleaflicker, MyFantasyLeague, Fantrax and Fantasy Premier League. Connection methods and history coverage vary; see the platform import guide for details. Public imports do not verify ownership. Yahoo Fantasy, ESPN, CBS Sports and NFL Fantasy account connections are not yet available. Never email provider passwords, API keys, authentication codes or cookies.

Following reports and abuse

Open the menu on a shared highlight or manager and choose Report, Mute or Block. Fantasy Index manager profiles also offer Report profile and Block manager in their menu; manage blocks from Fantasy Index → Blocked managers. A reported highlight is hidden from your feed; reporting a manager also mutes them. Reports are stored for operator review. For additional context or help, email support@fantasylockerapp.com with the FantasyLocker username and a description. Please do not send passwords or login codes.

Purchases and restoration

Open Profile → Your plan to view membership, restore purchases or manage subscriptions. Use the same FantasyLocker account used for the original purchase. Apple displays the current price and renewal period before checkout. Contact support if a completed purchase does not unlock its benefits; do not send card information or purchase account passwords.

Live AI image generation and AI credit checkout are currently unavailable. Native trophy styles and story backgrounds do not require an AI purchase.
