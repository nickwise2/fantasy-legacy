Support

Help with your Fantasy Legacy account, fantasy history imports, privacy, or app issues.

Contact support

Email support@fantasylegacy.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, or private API keys.

Account and privacy

Account deletion can be initiated from within the Fantasy Legacy app. For privacy questions or data-access requests, email support@fantasylegacy.com.

Read the Privacy Policy

Fantasy platform imports

Fantasy Legacy may connect to supported third-party fantasy platforms. Availability and imported history depend on the provider and the data the provider makes available.
