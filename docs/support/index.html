Sign in and confirm your email

Which account do I use?

Sign in with the email address and password for your FantasyLocker account. Your FantasyLocker username and your fantasy-platform username are not your sign-in email. If you used a +alias when registering, enter that full email address.

My confirmation link says it was used or expired

If you have already confirmed your email, return to FantasyLocker and choose Sign in. A confirmation link is not needed each time you open the app. Otherwise, request a new confirmation email and open the newest individual message, checking its recipient and time. Older messages may be grouped in the same email conversation.

The email has not arrived

Check your email address, Spam and Junk, and search for messages from support@fantasylockerapp.com. Avoid requesting several links in a row. If the newest link still fails, contact support with the time and exact error message. Never forward a confirmation link or authentication code.

Connect your leagues

Open Profile → Settings → Connected accounts, choose a supported platform, check the profile or team preview and confirm your selection.

  • Sleeper: use your Sleeper username for available football and basketball history.
  • Fantrax: use a public league link and select your sport, season and team.
  • Fleaflicker: find your account with its email address or user ID, then confirm the matching profile.

Fantrax and Fleaflicker provide available season standings, records and points. Deeper matchup and transaction history depends on Sleeper coverage. Public imports do not prove ownership or qualify a result for verified rankings. See the full import guide.

MyFantasyLeague, Fantasy Premier League, Yahoo Fantasy, ESPN, CBS Sports and NFL Fantasy account connections are not currently available.

Find or correct your history

Locker shows your current activity. Leagues contains league seasons, standings and available rivalries. Career brings your available records together across connected leagues.

A season, championship or matchup is missing

Check that you selected the right provider account, league, season and team. History depends on what the provider makes available. A regular-season first-place finish alone does not establish a championship. Missing information is not the same as a zero.

If an import is still running, allow it to finish before retrying. Do not delete your account or repeatedly remove and reconnect a league to fix a loading problem.

A record looks wrong or duplicated

Open the affected season in Career and choose Report a data issue, or email support with the platform, league, sport, season and the result you expected. Include a screenshot with unrelated personal information hidden. Reports preserve the current record while the source information is reviewed.

Purchases, cancellation and restore

I paid, but Pro or an item is missing

  1. Check the email for the FantasyLocker account you are signed into.
  2. Use the same Apple Account and FantasyLocker account used at checkout.
  3. Open Profile → Your plan → Restore purchases once and wait for the result.
  4. If the item is still missing, contact support with the product name, purchase time and exact message. If your build offers Purchase details, include its account reference and status.

You do not need to buy the same item again to restore it. An item linked to another FantasyLocker account belongs to the account used at checkout; changing your Apple Account alone does not move it.

I cancelled, but I still have Pro

Cancelling renewal keeps access until the subscription expires. An active renewing subscription should show Renews; a cancelled subscription that still has time remaining should show Expires. After expiry, the free Locker remains available. Restore does not renew an expired subscription.

Use Your plan → Manage subscriptions to view Apple's current subscription status. If the app and Apple disagree, reopen the app, try Restore once and contact support if the mismatch remains. Apple's cancellation instructions.

What if a payment is pending or fails?

Check Apple's purchase sheet or purchase history. A pending or unsuccessful payment may not unlock the offer. Do not keep purchasing to clear an error; contact support if Apple shows a completed purchase without access.

Does Founding Member cancel my subscription?

No. Founding Member is a one-time purchase. If you also have a Monthly or Annual subscription, manage that renewal separately through Apple.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store refund requests and determines eligibility. Follow Apple's refund instructions. Deleting FantasyLocker or your app account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Notifications

If your build includes Notifications, open Profile → Settings → Notifications, choose your alerts, tap Save and allow notifications when iOS asks. Also check iPhone Settings → Notifications → FantasyLocker.

Check your selected alert categories, timezone, quiet hours and iPhone Focus settings. A saved preference and permission on this iPhone are both needed. Not every imported event produces an alert, and availability varies by build, sport and provider. If the page says it could not connect, use its retry option and include the exact message when contacting support.

Sharing, reporting and blocking

Following and an owner's sharing settings control access to shared history. Connecting a league does not make every manager's private history visible.

Open the menu on a shared highlight, post or manager and choose the available Report, Mute or Block option. A report flags content for review; it does not itself establish a violation. You can also email support with the username, content and approximate time.

Use Blocked managers in Following or Fantasy Index to unblock someone. Unblocking does not automatically restore an old follow connection. Never send passwords or private account-access information in a report.

Export, remove a connection or delete your account

  • Export: Profile → Settings → Your data → Export profile and history.
  • Remove a connection: Settings → Connected accounts → the relevant connection.
  • Delete your FantasyLocker account: Settings → Delete account, then review the confirmation carefully.

Account deletion is different from signing out and may permanently remove your information. It does not cancel an Apple subscription. For privacy or access requests, contact support. Read the Privacy Policy.

Still need help?

Email support@fantasylockerapp.com.

Include:

  • Your FantasyLocker username or account email.
  • App version/build, iPhone model and iOS version. In TestFlight, use the build shown there.
  • What you tried, what happened, and the approximate time and timezone.
  • For imports: provider, league, sport and season. For purchases: product name and status.
  • A screenshot with unrelated personal information hidden, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, confirmation links, verification codes, API keys, cookies or payment-card details. Avoid reinstalling or deleting your account as a first troubleshooting step.
