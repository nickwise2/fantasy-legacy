Bring your history into your Locker

Five platforms in the beta

FantasyLocker currently offers import connections for Sleeper, Fleaflicker, MyFantasyLeague, Fantrax and Fantasy Premier League. The connection method, available seasons and depth of detail differ by platform.

Connect your account

In FantasyLocker, open Profile → Settings → Connected accounts and choose a platform. Confirm the returned account, team or manager before importing. Public imports do not prove ownership; ownership verification is separate from importing history.

Imported years and statistics depend on what the provider returns. A connection does not guarantee every past season, matchup, championship, trade or waiver transaction. Missing outcomes stay unknown.

Sleeper

Football and basketball · Username connection

Enter your Sleeper username, check the returned profile, and choose “Yes, import my history.” No Sleeper password or name change is needed to import public league history. The connection checks supported football and basketball history for that username. Available league seasons are imported together. Football supports deeper weekly comparisons; basketball detail depends on the records Sleeper supplies.

You do not need to reconnect the same username for each supported sport. In Accounts, you can pause updates, refresh history, remove a connection, or optionally verify ownership for rankings and badges.

Fleaflicker

Football, basketball, baseball and hockey · Email or user ID

Enter your Fleaflicker email or numeric user ID, confirm the returned account and import available seasons. The connection can bring in provider-returned standings, win/loss records, points and league settings. Regular-season rank is not treated as proof of a championship.

Imports stay private and ownership unverified. Use “Refresh Fleaflicker history” in Accounts to update the connection. History discovery checks up to 12 seasons; actual coverage depends on the provider.

MyFantasyLeague (MFL)

Football · League, season and franchise

Enter your MFL league link or league ID and season, then choose the franchise returned by the provider. Each connection covers one franchise in one league season. Public leagues do not need a credential; private leagues can use the league-bound owner API key from MFL’s Help → Developer’s API screen.

The import includes available records, points, standings and league settings. Refresh is manual; a private league asks for its owner key again because FantasyLocker does not retain it. Imported ownership remains unverified, and historical-season availability varies.

Fantrax

Public league seasons · League link and team

Choose the league sport, enter an official Fantrax league link or league ID, select the returned team and confirm the import. The sport selector includes football, basketball, baseball, hockey and soccer; the records available depend on the league and Fantrax response.

Each connection imports one team in one league season, including available standings records, points and rank. It does not infer championships or playoff finishes from regular-season standings. Refresh the connected season manually in Accounts.

Private Fantrax leagues are not supported by this connection. Public imports remain private in FantasyLocker and do not verify team ownership. No Fantrax password, cookie or private-session token is requested.

Fantasy Premier League (FPL)

Soccer · Public manager link or entry ID

Enter your official FPL manager link or entry ID, check the returned manager and import the public history. This connection brings in available season points and overall-rank history, with manual refresh in Accounts.

FPL overall rank is not a private-league finish. This import does not establish mini-league championships, head-to-head results or verified ownership; the imported manager history stays private.

Connections not yet available

Yahoo Fantasy: the secure sign-in integration is prepared, but provider access approval and live-account validation are still pending. A Yahoo option in Accounts does not mean a connection has been enabled.

ESPN, CBS Sports and NFL Fantasy: account connections are not available in the beta. Their presence in the platform list does not indicate a working import.

Your personal export

You can save a copy of your existing history in Settings. That copy cannot verify ownership or connect a provider account. Never share passwords, login codes, cookies or tokens.
