FantasyLocker (“FantasyLocker,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a fantasy sports career and legacy platform that allows users to create a persistent fantasy profile, connect supported fantasy platforms, import historical fantasy data, and view career statistics, records, leagues, seasons, rivalries, accolades, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the FantasyLocker mobile application, website, and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, username, profile information, account credentials, and other information you choose to provide.

When you connect or import data from a supported fantasy platform, such as Sleeper, we may collect fantasy-related information associated with that account. This may include provider account identifiers, usernames, leagues, teams, seasons, rosters, drafts, matchups, transactions, standings, records, and other fantasy sports history made available through the applicable provider.

We may also collect technical information related to your use of FantasyLocker, including device information, operating system version, application version, IP address, diagnostic information, authentication events, error logs, and information used to maintain the security and reliability of the service.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • create and maintain your FantasyLocker account;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • import, normalize, and display fantasy sports history;
  • create career statistics, records, accolades, rivalries, timelines, and other FantasyLocker features;
  • associate fantasy platform identities with the correct FantasyLocker user;
  • prevent duplicate or fraudulent identity claims;
  • operate, maintain, debug, and improve the service;
  • communicate with you about your account or the service;
  • investigate abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Public Profile Information

FantasyLocker is designed around persistent fantasy sports identity and career history.

Certain information may be displayed publicly or to other FantasyLocker users depending on the features and privacy controls available within the service. This may include your display name, username, profile image, leagues, seasons, fantasy teams, career records, statistics, championships, awards, accolades, rivalries, and other fantasy sports information.

We will not intentionally display your email address or authentication credentials publicly.

Fantasy Platform Connections

FantasyLocker may allow you to connect or identify an account from a third-party fantasy sports provider.

We may receive information from those providers through their APIs or other authorized integrations. The information available to us depends on the applicable provider and its policies.

FantasyLocker is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper or other third-party fantasy platforms unless explicitly stated.

Your use of third-party services is also governed by the privacy policies and terms of those providers.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate FantasyLocker.

For example, we currently use Supabase for services that may include authentication, databases, server infrastructure, and related backend functionality.

These service providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services to us, subject to their applicable agreements and privacy practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information:

  • to service providers that help us operate FantasyLocker;
  • when you direct us to share information;
  • when information is intentionally made public through FantasyLocker features;
  • when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of FantasyLocker, our users, or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction; or
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide FantasyLocker, maintain historical fantasy records, satisfy legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Because FantasyLocker is designed to preserve long-term fantasy sports history, certain fantasy career records may be retained for extended periods unless deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law and our product functionality.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your FantasyLocker account and associated personal information.

Where required by Apple App Store policies and applicable law, FantasyLocker will provide a method for initiating account deletion from within the application.

Some information may be retained where legally required or where necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Information involving other users, shared leagues, historical matchups, or league records may be anonymized or retained in a manner that no longer identifies you where complete deletion would affect the records of other users.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

FantasyLocker is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of certain information.

You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise applicable privacy rights, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make material changes, we may update the effective date and provide additional notice where appropriate.

Your continued use of FantasyLocker after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy where permitted by law.
