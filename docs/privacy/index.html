FantasyLocker (“FantasyLocker,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a fantasy sports career and legacy platform that allows users to create a persistent fantasy profile, connect supported fantasy platforms, import historical fantasy data, and view career statistics, records, leagues, seasons, rivalries, accolades, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the FantasyLocker mobile application, website, and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, username, profile information, account credentials, and other information you choose to provide.

When you connect or import data from a supported fantasy platform, such as Sleeper, we may collect fantasy-related information associated with that account. This may include provider account identifiers, usernames, leagues, teams, seasons, rosters, drafts, matchups, transactions, standings, records, and other fantasy sports history made available through the applicable provider.

We may also collect technical information related to your use of FantasyLocker, including device information, operating system version, application version, IP address, diagnostic information, authentication events, error logs, and information used to maintain the security and reliability of the service.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • create and maintain your FantasyLocker account;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • import, normalize, and display fantasy sports history;
  • create career statistics, records, accolades, rivalries, timelines, and other FantasyLocker features;
  • associate fantasy platform identities with the correct FantasyLocker user;
  • prevent duplicate or fraudulent identity claims;
  • operate, maintain, debug, and improve the service;
  • communicate with you about your account or the service;
  • investigate abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Private dues and returns

If you use dues tracking, we store the league season, currency, fee and amount returned that you enter, linked to your account. We use these amounts to show your private profit, loss and ROI. They do not appear on public profiles or affect FantasyIndex. We do not collect bank or card details or process payments through this feature.

You can clear amounts and save, include them in your account export, or remove them by deleting the associated imported history or your account.

Sharing controls

Your imported career records remain private unless you choose a sharing feature. Fantasy Index stats publication is a separate choice described below. Following is optional: joining makes your name and username searchable by signed-in members. Follow requests require your approval. Only posts, replies and season highlights you explicitly publish are shown to approved followers within the applicable conversation audience. Those cards include your name, username, year, sport, provider, record and finish; they exclude league names, opponents, photos and financial entries. Provider accounts are connected separately using provider identifiers; FantasyLocker does not discover accounts by searching emails or phone numbers.

Settings lets you remove a provider connection and its imported private history, or delete your account. Settings also offers a JSON copy of your profile, connected account identifiers, current career records and data-issue reports. This copy excludes credentials, internal logs and historical snapshots and is not a restore file.

Authorized file imports remain self-reported. Where an official connection is enabled, authorization takes place with the provider. Do not submit provider passwords, login codes or cookies.

Profile and trophy photos

A profile photo you select is uploaded to private account storage to display within your own profile. It is not included in the current public stats directory or Following profile projection.

You can add a league award image or trophy photo to a season. The image is stored privately with your account and can appear in championship share-card previews. Adding artwork does not change your results or award a title. You can replace or remove it from the season’s trophy page. Account deletion removes uploaded images; your account export includes the season and private image reference.

Share card images

When you create an image to share through the device share sheet, the preview shows the information included in that image. Depending on your selection, this can include manager and opponent display names, records, league names, championship years and trophy photos you added. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and complete sharing. This is separate from Following, where selected highlights keep league and opponent names private.

Sleeper username imports

When you confirm a Sleeper profile, we save its public provider identifier and league history privately in your Locker. This does not verify ownership. Separate ownership verification is required for verified rankings and badges. You can pause refreshes or remove the account in Accounts. Profile-confirmation tokens expire after 15 minutes.

Invite friends opens the device share sheet. FantasyLocker does not upload your address book or send invitations automatically; you choose the recipients and complete sharing.

Following and shared highlights

We store participation preferences, follow requests and approvals, shared season references, reactions, mutes, blocks and submitted reports to provide Following and address abuse. Mutes and blocks retain the name shown when you saved them so you can manage your list. They are private. Members can remove highlights and reactions, remove followers, mute, block or report from within Following. Reports are made available to authorized operators for review; they are not published.

Leaving Following hides your shared highlights and removes connections. Rejoining does not restore followers. Source corrections or loss of source verification can hide previously shared highlights until you choose to share the corrected season. Account deletion removes social rows associated with your account. Your account export includes your social settings, relationships, highlights, reactions, mutes, blocks and submitted report information, excluding internal moderation notes.

For help or abuse reports, contact support@fantasylocker.com.

Public Profile Information

FantasyLocker is designed around persistent fantasy sports identity and career history.

The information other members can see depends on the sharing option you choose. Following shares only the content you publish with its approved audience. Fantasy Index stats profiles show the eligible information listed below to signed-in members after you opt in. Private imported history, financial entries, uploaded photos and account credentials are not automatically published when you register or import a username.

We will not intentionally display your email address or authentication credentials publicly.

Fantasy Platform Connections

FantasyLocker may allow you to connect or identify an account from a third-party fantasy sports provider.

We may receive information from those providers through their APIs or other authorized integrations. The information available to us depends on the applicable provider and its policies.

FantasyLocker is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper or other third-party fantasy platforms unless explicitly stated.

Your use of third-party services is also governed by the privacy policies and terms of those providers.

Fantasy Index stats profiles

Publishing a stats profile is optional and separate from Following. If you opt in, signed-in members can see your display name, username, verified career totals, Fantasy Index and confidence, supported sports, season league names and results, and summaries of available roster-management evidence. Raw provider responses, email, provider credentials, photos and private dues entries are not included. You can withdraw publication in LockerRoom → Fantasy Index → Manage my public stats. Blocks and participation suspensions restrict visibility.

Purchases and optional AI

Apple processes in-app purchases. FantasyLocker uses verified purchase and entitlement information associated with your app account to enable purchased benefits; we do not receive your card number. Payment amounts and subscription availability are provided by Apple.

Live AI image generation and AI credit checkout are currently disabled. If activated in a later release, requested design instructions will be sent to the disclosed image provider only when you choose to generate a paid design. Current native trophy styles and animated story backgrounds do not send prompts to an AI service. OpenAI is the configured future image provider; fantasy history and login credentials are not required for design generation.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate FantasyLocker.

For example, we currently use Supabase for services that may include authentication, databases, server infrastructure, and related backend functionality.

Apple provides purchase handling and optional push delivery. Sleeper supplies supported fantasy history; available sports and detail depend on its data. ESPN scoreboard data can supplement NFL game status. Provider authorization, when available, occurs on the provider’s own site. Legal and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages.

These service providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services to us, subject to their applicable agreements and privacy practices.

Optional notifications

If you allow notifications, we store the push token, an installation identifier and notification preferences linked to your account so Apple can deliver relevant notifications. You can manage notification preferences in the app or disable permission in iOS Settings. We do not use this identifier for advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information:

  • to service providers that help us operate FantasyLocker;
  • when you direct us to share information;
  • when information is intentionally made public through FantasyLocker features;
  • when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of FantasyLocker, our users, or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction; or
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide FantasyLocker, maintain historical fantasy records, satisfy legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Because FantasyLocker is designed to preserve long-term fantasy sports history, certain fantasy career records may be retained for extended periods unless deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law and our product functionality.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your FantasyLocker account and associated personal information.

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account to initiate permanent deletion in the app. Deletion revokes sessions, removes private uploaded photos and personal records, and removes your posts and social relationships. Shared competitive history may remain in an anonymized form. Deleting your app account does not cancel an Apple subscription: cancel renewal in Apple’s subscription settings to avoid further charges. Immediate account deletion remains available.

Some information may be retained where legally required or where necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Information involving other users, shared leagues, historical matchups, or league records may be anonymized or retained in a manner that no longer identifies you where complete deletion would affect the records of other users.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

FantasyLocker is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
