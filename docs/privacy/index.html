Your data & your choices

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains how FantasyLocker handles information when you use our iPhone app, website and support services.

We do not sell your personal information, run third-party advertising, or use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

We collect information to operate your account, import and display fantasy history, personalize your experience, provide social features, and keep the service safe.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile: your email address, name, username, account identifiers and authentication information. Optional profile details include a bio, hometown, social handles, favorite teams and sports, and fantasy preferences. Hometown is a place you enter; we do not request GPS location.
  • Fantasy history: connected provider identifiers and available league, team, roster, draft, matchup, scoring, transaction and season records. This may include other managers’ identifiers and results that form part of your shared league history. Connection credentials or access tokens are handled when required by an enabled provider connection. Do not send provider passwords or login codes to support.
  • Content and relationships: posts, replies, reactions, shared highlights, selected profile and trophy photos, follow requests and follower relationships, blocks, mutes, privacy choices and saved preferences. We access photos you choose to upload, not your entire photo library. We do not request your phone’s address book; our social graph consists of relationships within FantasyLocker.
  • Activity and notifications: feed-read markers, interaction and synchronization records, notification preferences, time zone, a notification installation identifier and an Apple push-notification token when registered. These support unread badges, delivery and service operation.
  • Optional league finances: league fees, payouts and currency that you enter for your own records. This feature does not collect payment-card or bank-account information or move money.
  • Support and safety: support emails, data-correction requests, abuse reports and relevant account or content identifiers.
  • Technical information: request and authentication logs, IP addresses, client and device information, timestamps, errors and synchronization diagnostics used to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.

Information stored with your account is linked to you. Some app settings and calculations remain only on your device.

How we use information

We use information to sign you in; import, reconcile and display history; calculate career and opponent statistics; personalize your Locker and feed; store your chosen content; deliver requested notifications; maintain purchases and access; respond to support; investigate reports; prevent fraud and abuse; and meet applicable obligations. Your chosen favorites, imported gameplay history and following relationships help determine the content you see.

We do not use this information for advertising, advertising attribution or cross-company tracking. Our transactional email configuration has open and click tracking disabled.

What other people can see

Profile visibility and LockerRoom sharing depend on the choices and controls shown in the app. Content you choose to share, your visible profile information and relevant manager relationships may be available to other users according to those controls. Imported league records may identify managers who participated in those leagues.

Review your profile and sharing settings before posting. You can remove your posts and use report, block and unblock controls. Blocking limits visibility and interaction within the app; it cannot retrieve a copy someone already saved or remove records held independently by a fantasy provider.

Services that help us operate

We use service providers for these purposes:

  • Supabase: account authentication, databases, photo storage, backend processing and operational logs.
  • Resend: transactional email such as verification and account invitations, including recipient, message and delivery information.
  • Porkbun and Gmail: forwarding and handling messages sent to our support address.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this website and public assets. The host receives request information, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. We do not add advertising cookies or third-party analytics to this site.
  • Apple: app distribution, TestFlight, in-app purchases and push delivery.
  • Supported fantasy and sports providers: the requests needed to retrieve league history, sports information, avatars and player images. For example, current app features use Sleeper and ESPN endpoints. Requests made directly from your device expose ordinary network information, such as its IP address, to the recipient.

Providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may be processed in the United States or other countries where those providers operate. External links, including social-profile links, open services governed by their own policies.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users and the service, or support a business transfer with appropriate notice and safeguards.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store payment processing; we do not receive your payment-card details. The app reads Apple-verified product and transaction information to determine access, and supplies an account identifier to associate a purchase with your FantasyLocker account. The current app performs purchase entitlement checks on your device. If you send purchase details to support, those details become part of your support request.

Deleting FantasyLocker or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage renewal through your Apple subscription settings.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information and history while needed to provide the service. Support, safety and operational records may be kept longer when needed to resolve issues, investigate abuse, meet legal obligations or maintain security. Hosting and email providers apply their own backup and log-retention schedules, so removal from active systems may not immediately remove every backup or delivery log.

Account deletion revokes sessions and removes your account, private uploads and associated personal records from active services. Shared league membership may be anonymized as “Deleted manager” to preserve other users’ league history. Deletion does not remove original records at a fantasy provider, copies held by other users, or Apple’s purchase records.

Your choices and requests

  • Edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • Use Profile → Settings → Your data → Export profile and history for a copy of supported account and history records. This export does not include every operational log or the image files themselves.
  • Manage or remove connections under Settings → Connected accounts.
  • Start account deletion under Settings → Delete account.
  • Control notifications in the app and iPhone Settings.

For help, access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, contact support@fantasylockerapp.com. We may verify your identity before acting. Rights and available exceptions depend on your location; we respond as required by applicable law.

Security, children and changes

We use access controls and other safeguards to protect information, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share passwords, verification codes or private credentials in posts or support messages.

FantasyLocker is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice of material changes when appropriate or required.
