Before you jump in

A few things to know.

How do I get the beta?

Install Apple’s free TestFlight app on your iPhone, then open our beta invitation and choose Install. TestFlight will show device compatibility and whether places are available.

Is FantasyLocker on the App Store yet?

Not yet. We’re preparing the public release. This website will link to the App Store once FantasyLocker is available; for now, use the TestFlight invitation.

Will everyone see my imported history?

Your account has privacy and sharing controls. Choose whether to make your profile public and whether to use LockerRoom sharing. Review what you share and who can see it. Read the privacy policy.

What about memberships and purchases?

Optional membership and cosmetic offers appear in the app. Check the offer and Apple’s purchase sheet for the current terms. TestFlight uses Apple’s test purchase environment; the public app will use real App Store purchases.

Can I help improve it?

Absolutely. Send feedback through TestFlight or email support@fantasylockerapp.com. Include your app version and a short description, without passwords or login codes.
